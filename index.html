Help & shortcuts

Getting started

  • Open a photo with Open, drag it onto the page, or paste it. Dropping or pasting while a photo is open adds it as a layer.
  • Your original is never changed. Every edit is a layer or a step you can undo.
  • Projects save to this browser about every three seconds. Save (Ctrl/Cmd+S) saves now.
  • Export PNG or JPEG at full resolution. Ctrl/Cmd+E exports PNG.
  • Nothing leaves your device. Everything stays in this browser.

Canvas tools

Use the toolbar on the photo, or the keys in brackets.

  • Select (V): the default. Click a layer in the Layers tab to choose it.
  • Move (M): drag an image layer to move it. Drag a corner to scale it, or the handle above it to rotate. Hold Shift while rotating to snap to 15°. Drag near the centre to snap to it.
  • Crop (C): drag the corners or edges, or drag inside to move the crop. Aspect presets hold their ratio.
  • Straighten (S): drag along a horizon. The slope sets the angle, up to ±45°.
  • Clipping (J): shows blown highlights in red and crushed shadows in blue.
  • Before: hold the Before button, or press \ to toggle the original.

Adjusting the picture

  • Tune: brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, highlights and shadows. Sliders change every selected layer. Double-click a slider to reset it.
  • Auto tone sets brightness and contrast from the image. Copy tune and Paste tune move settings between layers.
  • Crop & frame: rotate, flip, straighten, correct perspective, set an aspect ratio, expand the canvas with a solid fill, and add a solid or Polaroid frame.
  • Effects: Vignette, Curves (five points), Details sharpen, Selective (set the radius and feather, then tap the photo), Looks (presets that replace the layer's tune), and Retouch clone (set a source, then paint with the clone brush).

Layers

  • Add an image, an adjustment layer, or a retouch layer.
  • Select several layers with Ctrl/Cmd-click, or a range with Shift-click. Ctrl+A selects all. Edits apply to the whole selection.
  • Group selected layers. Clicking one member selects the group.
  • Each layer has a name, visibility, lock, opacity, blend mode, and position and scale for image layers.
  • Layer style: drop shadow, outer glow, stroke and colour overlay. Copy style and Paste style move them between layers.
  • Mask: add a mask, or use a linear or radial gradient. Edit mask paints (reveal) or erases (hide). Invert flips it.

Tips

  • For Selective, set the radius and feather before you tap the photo.
  • Clone copies what is beneath the retouch layer. Set the source first, then turn on the clone brush.
  • Looks replace the layer's tune values. Auto tone and sliders can refine them afterwards.
  • Safari shows shadows and glows without blur in older versions. Newer versions match Chrome.
  • HEIC photos don't open in most desktop browsers. Convert them to JPEG or PNG first.
